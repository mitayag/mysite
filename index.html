About Me

My story

Currently the Dean of the School of Computing at Holy Angel University (Angeles City, Philippines). Former IT Program Chairperson and full-time faculty of its computing programs, and guest lecturer at the HAU Graduate School teaching cyber security subjects — ethical hacking and forensics.

Extensive experience in application development — desktop, web and mobile — across a multi-platform environment. Served as MIS Director at Systems Plus College Foundation, in charge of the day-to-day MIS operations and the school's IT infrastructure, and lectured at The Future University (Khartoum, Sudan), Baliuag University and Angeles University Foundation.

I have 4 children from my first wife — Mary Abigail, Mary Angel-lyn, Mary-Adelaine and Albert — and I'm happily married to my second wife whom I love so much… JENNIFER E. TAYAG ❤
